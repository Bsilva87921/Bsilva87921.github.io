What the data shows

    How I'd use this as a business analyst at Axiom Bank

    Axiom Bank does not appear among the 269 companies in this data. The value is learning where larger banks struggle before those problems reach a growing community bank.

    1. Map and measure

    Map the deposit, card dispute and fraud-claim processes end to end. Set baselines: complaints per 1,000 accounts, days to resolve, and share resolved on first contact.

    2. Define and test

    Write requirements for clear hold notices, self-service card controls and a faster unauthorized-transaction claim workflow, then prove each change through UAT before release.

    3. Track and improve

    Build a complaint scorecard by issue and channel, review it monthly with operations and product, and tie every fix to a measured drop in complaints.

    This is the same approach I used at JPMorgan Chase, where root-cause analysis on rising handle time led to targeted coaching and a call-type dashboard that cut handle time 30 seconds and saved $20M.
