Privacy

Probable Sports Lab and Probable Atlas · apps and websites by Bruno Silva · updated November 3, 2026

What the apps collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The apps do not collect, store or share personal information on any server.

What stays on your device

Your settings (light or dark theme, the last tab you used, your chat preferences), the teams, markets and countries you follow, your recent searches and the latest readings are saved in the app's local storage on your device so the app opens where you left it, including offline. You can clear them by deleting the app or clearing its data.

Alerts

Alerts about what you follow are worked out on your device from the same public data the pages show. If you turn them on, they appear as notifications from your browser or as local notifications from the app; nothing is sent to a server, and you can turn them off in your device settings at any time.

Share cards

Share images are drawn on your device. They are only sent where you choose to send them through your device's share sheet, or saved where you download them.

Network requests

  • Sports Lab reads public scoreboards, standings, stats and news from ESPN's public web APIs, and the track record and headlines file from bsilva87921.github.io.
  • Atlas reads its data files (IMF, World Bank, FRED, Yahoo Finance, Shiller, French) and its headlines file from bsilva87921.github.io.
  • News: headline pictures load from the publishers' image servers. Tapping a headline opens it in the reader on the page; the full article opens the publisher's own page (inside the app, in an in-app browser), which has its own privacy policy.
  • Ask AI answers with Claude, Anthropic's AI model, when the site's Claude service is on: your question, the conversation so far and the page data needed to answer it are sent through that service to Anthropic's API, and the service stores none of it. When the service is off or busy, a built-in analyst answers on your device instead and nothing leaves it. If you choose to use Claude with your own Anthropic API key, questions go from your device to Anthropic under your own account and the key is kept only on your device.

Children

The apps are general-audience analytics tools and are not directed at children.
