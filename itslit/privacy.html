Privacy and house rules

Plain words about what ItsLit keeps, why, and how readers treat one another. Last updated November 27, 2026.

In short: reading ItsLit needs no account. If you join, we keep only what the club needs: your name for the Salon, your email, your shelf and what you post. We never sell it, and you can delete your account at any time from the account panel.

Who we are

ItsLit Reading Club is an online reading club for classic literature. Questions about this notice or your information: use the reply address on any Gazette letter.

What we keep, and why

  • Your account: your email address, the display name you choose, and your profile picture if you sign in with Google. We use them to run your account; your display name and picture appear next to your posts.
  • What you post in the Salon, and the posts you cheer or report. Posts are public to everyone who visits.
  • Your shelf (want to read, reading, finished) and any waitlists you join. These are private to you, though the editors see waitlist totals.
  • The Gazette: if you subscribe, your email address, so we can send you our letters. Every letter includes a way to unsubscribe.
  • On your device only: your theme, filters, drafts and Matchmaker answers are kept in your browser and never sent to us.

Shelley, our AI librarian

When you ask Shelley a question, your message and the recent conversation are sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, to write the answer. We do not keep a copy of your conversations, and Shelley never asks for personal information, so please don't share any. Like any AI, Shelley can make mistakes: check important facts against the book itself. Shelley helps you understand and discuss books; she won't write work for you to hand in.

Who helps us run ItsLit

We use a few trusted services, each only for its job: Google Firebase (accounts and the club's database), Cloudflare (the connection to Shelley), Anthropic (Shelley's answers), GitHub Pages (hosting this website) and Google Fonts (typefaces). If we send the Gazette through an email service such as beehiiv, or take payments through Stripe, they handle your email address or payment details for that purpose only. We never see or store your card number.

Age

ItsLit accounts are for readers aged 13 and over. Anyone can read the Library without an account. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has made an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • Change your display name, or delete your account, from the account panel (top right on the website, the person icon in the app). Deleting removes your account, your shelf, your waitlists and everything you posted.
  • Unsubscribe from the Gazette with the link in any letter.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct or delete it, at the contact above.

House rules for the Salon

  • Be kind. Disagree with ideas, never with people. No insults, harassment or hate.
  • Guard the endings. Tick “Contains spoilers” when you discuss twists or endings.
  • Keep it about books and keep it suitable for readers aged 13 and up.
  • Keep personal details private: yours and everyone else's.
  • No advertising or spam. Use Report on anything that breaks these rules; the editors may remove posts and accounts that do.

Changes

If we change this notice in a way that matters, we will say so in the Gazette and update the date above.
